Non-DeepSeek OpenRouter models (e.g. `openai/gpt-4o`) built through `OpenRouterDeepseekProvider` no longer receive a DeepSeek-derived `max_price` ceiling or `order: ["DeepSeek"]` pin — only `allow_fallbacks`. Previously the provider unconditionally stamped a DeepSeek-tuned price ceiling on every model, so any non-DeepSeek model whose actual cost exceeded the ceiling (e.g. `openai/gpt-4o` at ~$2.50/$10 per 1M tokens vs. the cheap-tier ceiling of $0.25/$0.70) was rejected by OpenRouter with `404 No endpoints found that satisfy the max price`.
